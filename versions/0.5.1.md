# Prison Planet 0.5.1

Target: Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge 21.1.77.

## Authored Landmark Intake

Four player-authored structure templates have been imported as packaged mod resources:

- `prisonplanet:landmarks/rustwarden_keep`
- `prisonplanet:landmarks/safe_house`
- `prisonplanet:landmarks/waystone1`
- `prisonplanet:landmarks/waystone2`

The source templates in the Minecraft save remain unchanged. Packaged copies are produced through `prisonplanet/tools/import_landmark_structures.py`, allowing future exports to be re-imported with the same loot wiring.

## Loot Content

- `prisonplanet:chests/safe_house_supplies` supplies food, torches, heat resources, basic equipment, and modest Condemned material finds.
- The empty barrel in `safe_house` now references the safe-house supply table in the packaged template.
- `prisonplanet:chests/rustwarden_trial_reward` rewards completion of the keep encounter with metals, local resources, and rare trial-key, diamond, or Suspended Brine bucket outcomes.
- All eight custom-mob trial spawners in `rustwarden_keep` now reference the Rustwarden reward table through their trial-spawner configuration.

## Placement Status

These authored NBT templates are packaged and loot-ready, but are not yet inserted into procedural world generation. This keeps landmark placement intentionally separate from loot/encounter authoring.
