# Prison Planet 0.5.8

Target: Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge 21.1.77.

## Authoritative Phase Behavior

The Condemned cycle is `192000` ticks total. Each phase is `48000` ticks, equal to two standard Minecraft days. All environmental block processing is limited to loaded, ticking chunks in the simulation area and all solar/cold exposure checks require `Y >= 50` and direct visibility to open sky above the tested position.

### Day: Ticks 0-47999

- Vanilla sky light is held at its bright daytime state for the entire phase. The custom cloudless sky shows its intimidating oversized sun; the sun remains visually animated across the sky, but ambient daylight does not prematurely cycle to darkness.
- During the first `200` ticks of Day, living hazard targets receive a grace period and thaw by `4` frozen ticks per tick instead of taking solar damage.
- After grace, a sky-exposed player or native hazard-aware mob without Fire Resistance is ignited for `3` seconds every `40` entity ticks. Covered or below-threshold entities are not ignited.
- Rotating chunk sampling processes `4` random surface columns per selected ticking chunk on a `20`-tick distributed sweep.
- If a sampled exposed surface block is vanilla `stone`, `cobblestone`, `granite`, `diorite`, or `andesite`, it has a `1 in 3` chance to become an actual lava source. Its exact former block type is stored for reformation later.
- No Condemned geology, ash deposit, ore, prison construction block, or decorative custom stone is eligible for this lava conversion.
- Sampled exposed surface water and Suspended Brine are replaced by air. Suspended Brine can also evaporate on its own random tick during Day with a `1 in 3` chance when exposed.
- A sampled exposed flammable surface block has a `1 in 4` chance to ignite a valid fire block directly above it.

### Sunset: Ticks 48000-95999

- No solar burn or freezing hazard is applied. Existing frozen ticks on affected entities fall by `4` each tick.
- Vanilla's skylight calculation is fed a noon-to-midnight celestial angle across the `48000` ticks of Sunset. It therefore follows one extended vanilla-style dimming transition, with vanilla's normal bright/dark clamping at the ends, rather than looping through ordinary day/night repeats.
- Every server tick, up to `32` tracked loaded lava sources made from vanilla stone are restored to their precise original stone type. Nearby flowing lava created from each restored source is cleared in a small local region.
- No new evaporation, solar ignition, snow placement, water freezing, snow melting, or legacy molten-surface conversion is run by the environmental surface handler during Sunset.

### Night: Ticks 96000-143999

- Vanilla sky light is held at its darkest night state for the entire phase. The custom sky is moonless, cloudless, and displays its dense star field.
- During the first `200` ticks of Night, living hazard targets receive a grace period and thaw by `4` frozen ticks per tick.
- After grace, sky-exposed players and non-immune native mobs are cold-checked every `10` entity ticks. A covered or below-threshold entity thaws by `4`; an exposed entity within a valid heat radius thaws by `40`; otherwise it gains `24` frozen ticks, capped at `140`.
- At at least `70` frozen ticks, the handler maintains invisible Slowness I for `60` ticks. At `140` frozen ticks it maintains invisible Slowness II for `60` ticks; vanilla powdered-snow freezing damage behavior is driven by the filled frozen-tick meter.
- Heat is checked in a radius of up to `12` blocks. Lava and the Phase Lantern warm within `12`; lit campfires and lit soul campfires warm within `10`; tagged lit furnaces, smokers, blast furnaces, tagged fire/light fixtures, and soul fire warm within `8`.
- The Frost Wraith, Furnace Warden, and Glacial Drifter are fully exempt from the environmental entity hazard handler.
- Snow processing is distributed across loaded ticking chunks on a `4`-tick sweep, processing `1` random exposed surface column per selected chunk. It requires low block light (`< 10`) and a biome position where vanilla `shouldSnow` permits snow.
- Exposed sampled water becomes `packed_ice`.
- Snow begins as a layer on open air; sampled snow layers gain one layer; eight-layer snow becomes a full `snow_block`. Later placements accumulate above full snow blocks, so exposed areas can build multi-block snow depth.
- No new stone melting, evaporation, or solar ignition occurs during Night. Any tracked loaded solar lava continues to reform, up to `32` origins per server tick.

### Sunrise: Ticks 144000-191999

- No solar burn or freezing hazard is applied. Existing frozen ticks fall by `4` each tick.
- Vanilla's skylight calculation is fed a midnight-to-noon celestial angle across the `48000` ticks of Sunrise. It therefore follows one extended vanilla-style brightening transition, with vanilla's normal dark/bright clamping at the ends.
- Rotating chunk sampling processes `4` random surface columns per selected ticking chunk on a `20`-tick distributed sweep.
- For each sampled top snow layer, exactly one layer is removed; a one-layer snow block space becomes air. A sampled full `snow_block` becomes seven snow layers, then continues melting on future samples.
- A sampled exposed `packed_ice` surface becomes vanilla water.
- Any tracked loaded solar lava that remains is restored at up to `32` origins per server tick.
- No new stone melting, evaporation, solar ignition, snow placement, or water freezing occurs during Sunrise.

## Lighting Fix

- Added a Condemned-only `DimensionType.timeOfDay` override through a NeoForge-loaded mixin.
- Vanilla ambient skylight, sky-darkening calculations, daylight sensors, and vanilla darkness queries now use the extended phase curve instead of repeating every `24000` ticks.
- Other Minecraft dimensions retain their normal time and lighting behavior.
- Artificial block light remains determined by placed light sources, as expected.
