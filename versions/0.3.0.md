# Prison Planet 0.3.0

Target: Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge 21.1.77.

## Material Expansion

This release adds 92 new dimension-specific blocks, each supplied with a dedicated pixel texture, blockstate, item model, and loot table, plus a `The Condemned` creative inventory tab.

- Geology (8): `Ashen Shale`, `Charred Basalt`, `Brimstone`, `Vitrified Rock`, `Sulfur Crust`, `Frostbitten Stone`, `Obsidian Slag`, and `Compacted Cinder`.
- Ores (8): normal and deepslate variants of `Glacial Shard`, `Sulfur`, `Ferric Scrap`, and `Ember Crystal` ore; three new mined material items accompany the existing Glacial Shard.
- Masonry and industrial decor (24): etched/riveted/soot-stained/frozen bricks, mosaics and runed tiles, wall and bulkhead panels, concrete, plates, drainage and cage flooring, insignia/carved blocks, pillars, support columns, and pipes.
- Builder shapes (18): slabs, stairs, and connecting walls for six major surface families.
- Connected barriers (10): six transparent iron-bar/pane designs and four industrial fence designs.
- Lights (8): warm, cold, warning, ceiling, beacon, and ritual fixture families; warm fixtures also count as night heat sources.
- Plants (8): ash, frost, ember, root, glassweed, moss, and fern surface growth adapted to Condemned soils.
- Falling deposits (8): gravity-affected ash, cinder, sulfur, glass sand, slag gravel, and powdered char.

## Behavior And Generation

- Walls, fences, stairs, slabs, bars, and panes use vanilla block behavior. Custom wall and fence tags allow proper neighbor connections, while transparent panes and bars use cutout models.
- Falling surface deposits use vanilla colored falling-block physics; plants validate against the new ash and cinder soils.
- New chunks now expose varied ash/sand caps, geological strata, sparse surface plants, four ore families across shallow and deep rock, and additional industrial structure cladding.
- Ore and plant placement uses seeded scatter rather than repeating diagonal placement patterns.

## Acquisition And Visuals

- Added 52 stonecutting conversions for construction families and connected barriers.
- Added recipes for all eight new light sources using mined dimension materials.
- All new blocks are visible in the dedicated creative tab for construction and testing.
- A generated texture catalogue preview is stored at `prisonplanet/reference/condemned_block_catalog_0.3.0.png`.

## Existing 0.2.0 Features Retained

The extended hazard cycle, sky-exposure behavior, real lava conversion and restoration, night snowfall/freezing, moonless hostile sky, Nether-style portals, and seeded megastructure terrain remain included.
