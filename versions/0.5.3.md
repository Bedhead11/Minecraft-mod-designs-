# Prison Planet 0.5.3

Target: Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge 21.1.77.

## Existing-World Landmark Retrofit

- Condemned chunks generated before landmark worldgen was introduced are examined once when loaded.
- Previously generated terrain now receives safe houses frequently, waystones semi-rarely, and Rustwarden keeps rarely, at deterministic positions based on the world seed.
- Rustwarden keeps retain their `1-20` block burial range; safe houses and waystones are placed on the surface.
- Newly generated chunks still use the native structure sets from `0.5.2`, retaining normal structure-start behavior and `/locate` support there.

## Template Commands

- Landmark templates are packaged under both their pool IDs and convenient root aliases.
- Manual testing now supports `/place structure prisonplanet:safe_house`, `/place structure prisonplanet:waystone1`, `/place structure prisonplanet:waystone2`, and `/place structure prisonplanet:rustwarden_keep`.
