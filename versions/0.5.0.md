# Prison Planet 0.5.0

Target: Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge 21.1.77.

## Suspended Brine

This release introduces `Suspended Brine`, an alien industrial coolant fluid native to the Condemned.

- It is a source/flowing fluid with a collectible bucket, but it is finite: placing or spreading it never creates renewable source blocks.
- Its propagation algorithm is inverted: streams travel upward, search laterally for upward openings, and gather as pools along ceilings.
- Its fluid volume shape and custom client renderer expose the lower liquid surface, so a ceiling pool reads correctly from a room beneath it.
- Its dark-teal animated liquid sprites, cyan underside rims, upward streaks, bucket texture, and submerged fog distinguish it from ordinary water.

## Dimension Integration

- Suspended Brine is not currently generated in terrain or structures; it is available by bucket for gameplay testing and later authored placement.
- Its thicker, slower spread and limited lateral range use lava-like fluid dynamics without making the coolant hot lava.
- Directly sun-exposed brine at or above the hazard elevation evaporates during the daytime phase; roofed coolant remains stable.
- It is intentionally not classified as water: it does not form infinite pools, hydrate, extinguish, support boating, or behave as a swimming liquid.

## Assets

- In-game fluid and bucket assets are reproducibly generated by `prisonplanet/tools/generate_fluid_assets.ps1`.
- The texture presentation sheet is stored at `prisonplanet/reference/suspended_brine_textures_0.5.0.png`.
