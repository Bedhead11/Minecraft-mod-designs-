# Prison Planet 0.5.2

Target: Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge 21.1.77.

## Landmark World Generation

- Safe houses generate frequently on the Condemned surface through a dedicated structure set with `14` chunk spacing and `7` chunk separation.
- Waystones generate semi-rarely on the surface with equal selection between `waystone1` and `waystone2`, using `32` chunk spacing and `12` chunk separation.
- Rustwarden keeps generate rarely with `64` chunk spacing and `24` chunk separation.
- A Rustwarden keep is projected to the terrain surface and sunk randomly `1-20` blocks underground, leaving it principally associated with surface exploration while allowing partial burial.

## Loot Rebalance

- Safe-house barrels now reliably contain coal, `4-16` sticks, torches, and food, with occasional potion ingredients and a rare Fire Resistance potion.
- Rustwarden trial rewards are now late-game loot: enchanted netherite gear, high-value enchanted books with `1-5` enchantment rolls, diamonds, netherite scrap, and ancient debris.

## Authored Assets

The four player-authored NBT templates remain under `prisonplanet:landmarks/`, with the existing embedded barrel and trial-spawner loot hooks used by naturally generated landmarks.
