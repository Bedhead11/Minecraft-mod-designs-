# Prison Planet 0.1.0

Target: Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge 21.1.77.

## Included

- Netherite-frame portal travel with a protected dimension arrival cell and surface-safe return placement.
- Server-authoritative four-phase, 192,000-tick cycle with transition events and initial hazard grace periods.
- Exposed daytime burning and nighttime freezing, including tagged heat sources and the craftable Phase Lantern.
- Reactive condemned surface stone that becomes molten during Day and solidifies during Sunset.
- Night snow/ice and Sunrise thaw simulation around active players.
- Procedurally carved prison decks, corridors, chambers, silos, surface breaches, and catacomb passages.
- Survival-facing recipes and drops for the first exploration loop.

## Roadmap Still Open

- Permafrost armor, Mending Flame UI, enchantment suite, villagers, custom mobs, and phase spawning.
