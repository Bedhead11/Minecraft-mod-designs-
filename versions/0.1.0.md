# Prison Planet 0.1.0

Target: Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge 21.1.77.

## Included

- Nether-style Netherite-frame portals with ten-block minimum frames, red-black animated surfaces, screen distortion, and matching return portals.
- Visible Minecraft day-time phases with transition events and initial hazard grace periods.
- Visible-time-aligned direct-sky daytime burning and nighttime freezing/snow, including tagged heat sources and the craftable Phase Lantern.
- Real liquid-lava solar melting for exposed vanilla stone pockets, with original stone reforming whenever daylight ends.
- Night snow/ice and Sunrise thaw simulation around active players.
- Natural ashen hills and valleys interlaced with connected prison decks, shafts, skylight breaches, transparent bars, and catacomb passages.
- Custom moonless Condemned sky with a massive hostile sun and dense nighttime stars.
- Survival-facing recipes and drops for the first exploration loop.

## Roadmap Still Open

- Authored Jigsaw structure pools, landmarks, custom biomes, and structure loot.
- Permafrost armor, Mending Flame UI, enchantment suite, villagers, custom mobs, and phase spawning.
