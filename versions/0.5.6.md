# Prison Planet 0.5.6

Target: Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge 21.1.77.

## Heavy Night Snowfall

- Night precipitation now follows vanilla's snow landing behavior: random loaded-chunk surface samples, motion-blocking height selection, survival validation, block-light suppression, and exposed-sky placement.
- Snow placement is approximately four times more frequent than vanilla's default precipitation placement opportunity while remaining batched to protect server tick performance.
- A full eight-layer snow block promotes into a solid snow block; new layers may then build on top, allowing deep multi-block drifts over a long Condemned night.
- Water exposed at the surface still freezes during the Night phase, and Sunrise still removes accumulated snow progressively from the top down.

## Testing

The custom Night phase begins at dimension time `96000`; use `/time set 96000` while inside the Condemned to test snowfall immediately.
