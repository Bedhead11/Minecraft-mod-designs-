# Prison Planet 0.2.0

Target: Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge 21.1.77.

## Included

- An extended 192,000-tick Condemned cycle: Day, Sunset, Night, and Sunrise each last 48,000 ticks, equal to two Minecraft day durations.
- Direct-sky hazards only above Y 50: exposed players burn during Day and freeze during Night unless protected by heat.
- Day solar effects: exposed overworld stone pockets become real liquid lava and restore after Day, exposed water evaporates, and exposed flammable surfaces ignite.
- Night storm effects: visible heavy snowfall in the custom cold biome, uncapped physical snow stacking across loaded block-ticking chunks, and exposed water freezing into packed ice.
- Neutral Sunset apart from lava restoration, followed by Sunrise thawing accumulated snow and exposed ice.
- Expanded warmth protection: ordinary heat sources reach 8 blocks, campfires reach 10 blocks, and lava or Phase Lanterns reach 12 blocks.
- Seed-aware natural ashen hills and valleys interlaced with connected multi-deck prison districts, grand tunnels, silos, breach courtyards, exposed meltable stone, and transparent bars.
- Custom moonless hostile sky with a massive sun and dense stars synchronized to the extended cycle.
- Nether-style Netherite-frame portals with ten-block minimum frames, red-black animation, portal distortion, and reusable matching return portals that do not rebuild the arrival chamber on repeat travel.
- Complete pickaxe harvest and self-drop coverage for the Condemned construction block set.
- World generation is applied to newly generated chunks; use a new world or unexplored chunks to see the redesigned seeded districts.
